2. What data we collect

In the beta signup form we may collect:

  • name / handle,
  • email address,
  • industry / professional role,
  • workflow and product needs (if provided voluntarily),
  • technical submission data (for example submission timestamp and server logs).

3. Purpose and legal basis

  • processing beta applications and contacting users about TIMEFLOW beta access,
  • organizing and prioritizing beta testing,
  • protecting the form against abuse and spam (legitimate interest).

The legal basis is user consent and, where applicable, the controller's legitimate interest in securing the form and operating the signup process.

4. Retention period

Beta application data is stored only as long as needed to handle the beta program and related communication, and no longer than 12 months, unless a longer retention period is required by law or you withdraw consent earlier.

5. Data recipients

Data may be processed by hosting or infrastructure providers that help operate the landing page and form submission backend, only to the extent necessary to provide the service.

6. Your rights

You may have the right to request:

  • access to your data,
  • rectification,
  • erasure,
  • restriction of processing,
  • data portability,
  • withdrawal of consent,
  • and the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority.

7. Voluntary data provision

Providing data is voluntary, but missing name/handle, email, industry, or contact consent may prevent us from processing the beta application.

8. Privacy contact

For privacy-related requests, contact the data controller at: office@conceptfab.com.
